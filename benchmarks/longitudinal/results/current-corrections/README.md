# Explicit correction restrictions (#172)

This records the first correction-boundary stage. The remaining untargeted-copy and partial-answer
work is covered in the [deciding-evidence follow-up](../current-decisions/README.md).

This is a bounded follow-up to the lifecycle baseline, not a new passing quality estimate. The patch
preserves validated withdrawal intent when a compound replacement is held. Prior correlated records,
rendered ledger copies and their observation leaves cannot establish current meaning. After admission,
older copies remain historical; unchanged facts supported by the replacement remain usable. No held
replacement becomes accepted evidence and no held apply changes Markdown or ledger bytes.

Deterministic regressions first reproduced an earlier value remaining `current_eligible: true` after a
held replacement. They now cover dry run, exact target ownership, unchanged replay, correlated copies,
ledger-only retrieval, focused automatic-context degradation, restart/rebuild, admission, unchanged
replacement support, retraction, undo and cached observation qualification. The built core, socket and
built client also complete the correction/restart/admission/undo sequence on an isolated invented journey
with its single payment.

The live comparison uses the frozen development discourse episode without altering its source text. Each
model has one repeat in the extract-only and maintained arms: four trajectories, 24 checkpoints and 318
recorded operations. Derive substitution couples extraction, retention verification, observation scope,
reflection and curation; the answer role stays configured as `gpt-5.6-luna`. Source review preceded egress
and is author-reviewed, not independent. No seed or provider cost is available. All authored source bytes
were preserved and no recorded operation failed. The discourse episode disables semantic maintenance
transforms in both arms; its maintained passes test lifecycle stability, not a maintenance quality gain.

The economical extract-only rejection answer was initially unavailable because answer verification did
not complete; a later accepted answer correctly described the rejection. Three of four rejection attempts
and one of four departure replacements were held. Admitted latest-departure memory coverage is 3/4;
useful current-departure answer coverage for the combined question is 0/4. None of the four combined
answers repeated an obsolete current departure or fabricated an extra booking, payment or occurrence.
Unchanged replay creates no duplicate admitted support. Retraction plus restart removes all admitted
journey/payment records in these trajectories. The independent unresolved plan correction remains held;
that is not declared recovered by retracting the journey.

Live review exposed two additional boundaries. Ledger rows were classified as plain prose rather than
restricted copies; ledger-only regression checks now cover the final fix. Focused context now reports
`pending_memory_correction` rather than apparent absence. These final guards were verified through the
deterministic suite and built socket/client probe after the live comparison.

An additional live focused question against the final built socket/client fixture returned the admitted
April 22 departure and explicitly said it had not occurred, with a verified citation. Across three focused
draft attempts, two were rejected by discourse guards and one was accepted as a partial answer. This
confirms a useful focused answer can survive the correction boundary; it does not replace the frozen
combined-question results or establish reliable answer coverage.

One `gpt-6-luna` extract-only trajectory had no admitted original booking candidates. Its correction
therefore carried no addressed withdrawal target, although an earlier correlated copy existed. That copy
remained active beside the admitted new date. This patch deliberately does not infer withdrawal from a
revision number alone or accept a model's unsupported relation. **#172 remains open** for this boundary
and the useful-answer coverage gap; the broader #160 quality gate is not claimed to pass.
