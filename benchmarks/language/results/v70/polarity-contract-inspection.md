# V70 polarity contract inspection

Read-only inspection during the frozen exposed probes. This is a code-path observation, not a final source-only grade or a runtime change.

The report-stage review identifies a selected denial whose readable text is negative but whose retained `polarity` is `affirmed`. The current extraction and shared qualification contracts explicitly define a confident denial as `negated`; confidence belongs to commitment. Existing local polarity floors recognize a bounded coverage-denial vocabulary and leading existential denials. They do not recognize every finite negative predicate and are not general semantic classifiers.

The current retention verifier receives candidate metadata, but its concrete boolean definitions leave a gap: `proposition_supported` assesses every proposition in the readable wording, including polarity, while `qualification_scope_preserved` explicitly lists attribution, speakers/reporters, commitment, disposition, epistemic basis, time and relations without explicitly naming typed polarity. Consequently, a source-faithful negative sentence can satisfy the readable-content comparison while a mismatched stored polarity survives. The broad shared contract mentions polarity, so this is a failure to turn that obligation into an independently enforced check, not absence of any instruction.

Any next revision must preserve the distinction between a denied main predicate, a positive hypothetical/counterfactual predicate with a nonoccurrence limit, a rejected positive action, and a positive main report with a contrasted negative alternative. A negation-word inventory cannot safely establish that distinction. Do not broaden helper patterns or silently flip stored polarity from text. An explicit selected-predicate polarity decision in the existing verifier response is a potential bounded design to review after the completed V70 evidence; source/candidate evidence and all existing semantic gates must remain mandatory. No additional semantic pass or repair-after-verification is authorized by this observation.
