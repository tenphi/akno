# Instruction-obligation review: `v3-dev-alternatives`

## Scope and method

This is a source-only rubric review. I read only the `v3-dev-alternatives` entries in `corpus.json` and `source-first-obligations.json`, plus `grading-contract.json`. I did not inspect runtime code, prompts, outputs, packets, grades, scores, blind mappings, or other reviews.

The caller's mission is to retain durable knowledge about Zephyr QX-100 while preserving all original qualifications. I therefore distinguish durable source content from an instruction that tells the retention system how to handle that content.

## Judgment

“Keep both alternatives for discussion” is **(b): a retention/discourse instruction fulfilled by preserving both explicitly unestablished hypotheses as discussion-qualified records**. It is not a separate durable proposition whose imperative wording must itself be restated in generated prose.

The sentence is grammatically and functionally an instruction. Its purpose is to prevent selection, collapse, or factual promotion of either alternative. Once both alternatives survive as hypotheses, remain mutually incompatible, remain unestablished, and retain their discussion status, the instruction has been obeyed. Requiring an additional durable statement such as “both alternatives are to be kept for discussion” would turn the retention command into knowledge about the command and would duplicate its operational effect.

The obligation document labels that sentence as a `retentionProposition`, but the same document's `coupledQualifications` and `requirements` show what the requirement substantively protects: both alternatives must remain hypotheses, neither may be selected or asserted as actual, and annual versus biennial must remain recurring service intervals rather than dates. Read consistently with the source and the caller mission, the third listed item is an operational retention requirement, not an independently reportable world proposition.

## Role of full-record metadata

Full-record qualification metadata counts in this judgment when it is part of the retained record being graded. In particular, `view: discussion` and `commitment: hypothetical`, together with preservation of both alternatives and their unestablished status, are evidence that the imperative has been fulfilled. The grading contract requires correct qualifications and distinguishes omitted independent neighbors from distortion of qualifications coupled to a requested claim; it does not require every qualification to be repeated verbatim in prose when the complete retained record carries it unambiguously.

Metadata cannot rescue missing substantive content. A record containing only discussion/hypothetical labels would still be incomplete if it omitted either interval, erased their incompatibility, selected one, or failed to preserve that neither is established. Likewise, prose that states an annual or biennial interval as fact would fail even if metadata elsewhere said `hypothetical`, because the record would be internally contradictory and would promote an unestablished alternative.

## Independently required durable content

The content that must survive is:

- Ada Marlow introduced two alternatives about Zephyr QX-100 warranty-service frequency.
- One alternative is annual service and the other is biennial service.
- The alternatives are competing or incompatible hypotheses.
- Neither alternative is established, selected, or the actual service interval.
- The intervals are recurring frequencies, not calendar dates.

Their discussion and hypothetical status must be preserved across the complete record. A separate prose restatement of the retention imperative is unnecessary if those requirements are satisfied. For the focused query, a useful answer must identify both annual and biennial service and keep them qualified as competing, unestablished hypotheses; attribution should also remain consistent with the source and the obligation's stated role requirement.
