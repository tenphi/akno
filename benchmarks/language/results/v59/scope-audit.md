# V59 fiction query-scope audit

I re-read the complete `v19-held-fiction` source, both bilingual questions, and the four produced English answers in the finalized selected V59 packet. I did not use gate outcome as a criterion.

The question is not asking whether Ada proposed a discussion or who proposed it. It already supplies that frame and asks for the object within it: **“What fictional axle-cap promise did Ada Marlow propose discussing for Zephyr QX-100?”** A natural concise answer can therefore state the requested promise without repeating the full relative clause from the question.

Each disputed answer begins with `In Ada Marlow's fictional example [about Zephyr QX-100]` and then supplies the complete requested object: Vulpine Mutual promises Bo Winters free axle-cap replacements during the first ten weeks of ownership, and the promise belongs only to fiction. The possessive associates the example with Ada in the same discourse, while the question itself supplies the proposing relation. In question-answer composition, this is responsive and does not leave the listener unable to identify which promise Ada proposed discussing.

This differs from an answer that changes the actor of a proposition it independently asserts, such as “According to Ada, Bo proposed it” or a passive proposal statement that is offered as a standalone account of who acted. It also differs from omitting part of the requested object, such as the promisor, recipient, benefit, ten-week limit, or fictional scope. None of those defects occurs here.
