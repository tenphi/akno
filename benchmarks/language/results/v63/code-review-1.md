# V63 code review — round 1

## Disposition

No actionable correctness defect found in the five-file prompt-only diff against the current HEAD.

The change is source-parametric rather than a lexical ban. Retention and answer generation only make the contractual qualifier explicit when the supplied source establishes that sense, and both explicitly preserve a source-supported physical condition or status display. The new text also says that nearby contract vocabulary alone is insufficient. That is the necessary boundary against turning domain co-occurrence into authority.

The verifier changes consistently treat the epistemic limit as a structured relation: actor, predicate, object/referent, and the proposition it qualifies. They do not let matching uncertainty vocabulary hide a changed object. The dimension guidance is coherent with the existing all-dimensions gate: a changed epistemic object makes `qualification_scope_preserved` false; it also makes `proposition_supported` false when the answer asserts that changed object, and makes `action_arguments_preserved` false when it changes the object of an examining/confirming action. The alignment instructions now require corresponding negative relations and mismatches, so they do not create an acceptance override.

Neutral record nonresolution remains distinct from personal nonverification. The added rule expressly permits human attribution to scope over a record-level statement without converting it into that person's ignorance, while retaining each actual personal limit and required report attribution. This also avoids requiring a repeated personal name in every clause.

For mixed citations, the wording requires comparison of every material selected limit against its counterpart but does not require every cited record to repeat limits supplied only by another record. Existing per-record alignment and complete-block interpretation remain in force. A jointly composed clause may therefore be assessed against each record's selected contribution without transferring epistemic subjects between records.

## Finite limits

- The improvement remains a fallible prompt contract; it is not a deterministic contractual-sense parser. Rare translations can still choose the wrong sense and be missed by the same verifier.
- One qualification alignment entry per cited record must summarize multiple epistemic limits when a record contains them. The new instruction makes those limits mandatory to assess, but the schema does not enumerate them separately.
- `reported contractual condition` and `сообщение об условии договора` are useful disambiguating examples, not prescribed surface strings. The surrounding text correctly allows other faithful expressions and preserves physical-state uses.

Prompt versions are bumped consistently for each changed runtime prompt: retention extraction `v46`, retention verifier `v31`, answer generation `v58`, and answer verifier `v39`. The changeset accurately states that schemas, calls, retries, and consistency gates remain unchanged.

## Final-diff addendum

The subsequent refinements resolve the ambiguity left in the initial wording without introducing a new authority path:

- A material epistemic limit is now defined as the complete scoped predicate—grammatical subject or experiencer, predicate, object/referent, and clause attachment. The examples distinguish reading terms, receiving confirmation of a report, and personally verifying a contractual condition rather than collapsing them under generic uncertainty.
- Generation may carry the contractual sense in the clause itself or through an unambiguous antecedent. This permits ordinary cohesive prose while still requiring the antecedent to derive from the selected source meaning; query wording, nearby contract vocabulary, and source metadata do not become evidence.
- The alignment guidance correctly distinguishes a record/note that is the grammatical epistemic subject from an outer human reporter. It also states that self-attested provenance alone does not require repeated speaker wording, while personal agency and actual report attribution remain required where selected.
- The coordinate rule now mirrors the existing schema exactly: `omitted` requires a null answer anchor, while a present changed/generalized counterpart uses its real anchor. This directly addresses the V62 row-99 malformed verdict without changing parsing or acceptance behavior. A structurally valid negative alignment remains an ordinary rejected block.

The V63 trial plan accurately records the preserved V62 evidence and correction, the prompt-only scope, unchanged source/selection/repair/routing authority, four version bumps, unchanged models/calls/retries/budgets/gates, and the limited meaning of compiled stubs. The benchmark expectation updates to answer generation `v58` and verifier `v39` match runtime constants.

Final disposition remains clean: no actionable blocker found. The finite limits from the initial review still apply; the added prose guides the existing model judgment and does not certify perfect antecedent resolution or enumerate multiple epistemic predicates in the schema.
