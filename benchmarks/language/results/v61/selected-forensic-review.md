## Bounded V62 design implications

### Retained ownership for a source-person device denial

The no-booking candidate is semantically sound; only routing loses it. Its raw subject, `Ada Marlow's device collection booking`, is an event/state phrase, while no supplied canonical page represents that subject. A bounded improvement should not make ownership infer that the unspecified device is Zephyr from adjacency. Instead, when the readable source has a first-person possessive generic device and a known source identity, generation should retain the proposition with the person as the subject/owner (`Ada Marlow — no collection of her device has been booked`) and propose that person's supplied canonical page if one exists. If no exact person profile is supplied, retain a complete eligible folder/slug proposal rather than borrowing a product page. Ownership and policy checks remain mandatory.

Necessary contrasts: explicit `my device` with a known speaker/person profile; anonymous `a device`; another person's device; two people in the same source; an explicit product identifier (which must keep product ownership); a booking made by an unspecified actor (do not invent Ada as booking actor); and no available person page, where proposed routing is allowed but a product fallback is not.

### Group-relative unknownness

The source says `Настоящие требования нам неизвестны`: unknown **to us**, without identifying Ada alone as the epistemic subject. Extraction should preserve that group-relative scope directly, e.g. `The actual requirements are unknown to the group/us`, and should not force a Zephyr subject identifier into metadata unless the readable deciding span binds it. The candidate can remain separately retrievable without copying the hypothetical rule into its proposition merely to satisfy subject validation.

A structural solution is to keep this group-relative limit as a qualifier in the hypothetical record, or retain it separately with an exact minimal deciding span and named antecedent only when the source supplies one. Here no canonical group identity is established, so a standalone candidate should remain `unresolved` with null routing rather than propose a group page. Do not rewrite `unknown to us` as Ada personally not knowing, global unknowability, or unknown product state. Full original-source semantic verification remains mandatory.

Necessary contrasts: `unknown to us`; `unknown to Ada`; `unknown` with no experiencer; `the device state is unknown`; `we do not know X` beside a separate product sentence; and a source that explicitly binds the group to a supplied subject. Repair must target the original failed position and preserve all already admitted candidates unchanged.

### Answer-side bounded corrections

For complete-record verification, original-frame material absent from the retained excerpt is interpretation context, not automatically selected output. A negative alignment must identify a restriction belonging to the retained proposition. Prefer canonical `По словам SOURCE` for named RU reporting instead of growing a list of passive synonyms. Extend the existing source-clock participial grammar to directly bound `от времени (этой|исходной) записи`, with device/processing/quotation/negation contrasts and mandatory unknown-clock plus semantic checks.

These changes need no new provider pass, retry, model, schema, budget, ownership bypass, or gate relaxation.
