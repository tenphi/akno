## Smallest justified next scope

The evidence supports four bounded changes, in priority order:

1. **Retention presentation contract:** permit one compact self-contained prose record containing short complete sentences within the unchanged 400-unit cap. Put personal reading/checking/confirmation limits in their own explicit-subject sentence, preserving possession versus performance predicates. Use a syntax-specific one-repair diagnostic. This addresses the report false hold without weakening semantic verification.
2. **Explicit counterfactual rendering:** direct Russian generation to prefer a complete `если бы` antecedent, subjunctive consequence, actual nonoccurrence, and not-current qualification, while inserting required source names byte-for-byte. Do not enumerate arbitrary nominal synonyms or relax the floor.
3. **Closed Russian source-clock construction:** admit the affirmative same-clause form `«следующий месяц» отсчитывается от недатированной первоначальной записи, а не от ... обработки` only with separate unknown-calendar evidence. Keep question, quote, conditional, denial, reversed direction, non-source event, and retraction negatives.
4. **Fiction composition:** when the focused question asks what promise Ada proposed discussing, require the selected proposition unit to retain Ada's proposal action together with the fictional promise. Neutral `According to Ada` is not an equivalent actor relation. Existing per-record citation and semantic checks should remain mandatory.

Generation guidance should continue to prefer active Russian coverage wording (`покрывается ли ремонт ...`) because the three exclusion holds are real role errors. The language gate correctly rejected untranslated `hinge-pin`; it should not be relaxed.

None of these findings justifies another model pass, retry, cap increase, source-authority substitution, or weaker semantic/language gate.
