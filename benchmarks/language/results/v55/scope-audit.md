# V55 report-scope and hypothesis-completeness audit

## Built report syntax

The built RU→RU inferred wording is:

> По словам Ada Marlow, передавшей сообщение Bo Winters, условия ...

The opening `По словам Ada Marlow` unambiguously makes Ada the outer source. Because `Bo Winters` is indeclinable, `сообщение Bo Winters` is formally case-ambiguous. The natural reading in this noun phrase is possessive/genitive: “Bo Winters's message,” so Ada is the person transmitting Bo's report. A recipient reading (“the message [to] Bo Winters”) is grammatically imaginable, but Russian would ordinarily signal it more clearly through word order or a preposition, such as `передавшей Bo Winters сообщение` or `сообщение для Bo Winters`. The answer's full context—Ada's lack of seeing the terms or receiving confirmation—also supports the relayed-source reading. I therefore retain the passing judgment. The ambiguity is stylistic and does not outweigh the dominant source-faithful parse.

The other built report rows make the chain clearer: `По словам Ada Marlow, Bo Winters сообщил...` directly names Ada as outer reporter and Bo as inner reporter; `По словам Bo Winters ... Ada Marlow лишь передала эти слова` names Bo as the attributed source of the embedded terms claim and Ada as its relay. Both preserve the original chain rather than claiming Ada originated the service statement.

## Selected report syntax

The selected EN→RU inferred wording is materially different:

> По словам Bo Winters, Ada Marlow передала сообщение о том, что ...

Here `По словам Bo Winters` scopes over the finite clause `Ada Marlow передала сообщение`: it says, according to Bo, Ada relayed the message. Nothing in the original establishes Bo as the outer source for Ada's act of relaying. The original instead has Ada directly recording that Bo told her the embedded claim. This is a definite reversal of the nested attribution structure, not merely the case ambiguity found in `сообщение Bo Winters`. The existing `sourceEntailed:false`, `qualificationPreserved:false`, and usefulness rejection remain warranted.

In this full sentence, neither phrasing would imply uncertainty about whether Ada proposed it, but the disputed wording is especially well anchored to proposed disposition and nonacceptance. It does not mechanically convert the embedded commitment from asserted to tentative.
