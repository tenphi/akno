# V45 connector-specificity consistency review

No receipt labels change.

The source hypothesis is `неплотно вставленный внутренний разъём`: the connector is faulted in how it was inserted or seated. The accepted forms `improperly inserted` and `неправильно вставленный` retain that insertion action and identify an improper seating condition. They do not reproduce the narrower adverb `неплотно` word for word, but in this fault frame they are ordinary, faithful translations of an incompletely or improperly seated connector. The contract requires preservation of the material mechanism rather than verbatim lexical identity, so those forms remain complete and useful.

`Неправильно установленный внутренний разъём` crosses the boundary because `установленный` describes installation more broadly and no longer binds the defect to insertion or seating. It can cover a wrong connector placement or another installation error that the source never proposed. The source still entails the weaker proposition that the connector was installed incorrectly, so `sourceEntailed: true` remains appropriate; however, the answer does not preserve the specific insertion-based competing hypothesis and therefore remains incomplete and not useful.

The consistent distinction is thus not merely loss of the word “loosely.” It is whether the rendering preserves the source’s insertion/seating action. Applying a rule that rejected every nonliteral loss of `неплотно` would also reject normal translations such as `improperly inserted` despite their preservation of the operative mechanism, which the semantic contract does not require.
