## Conclusion

No grade correction is warranted for `v19-held-undated`, run 2. The retained record, its retrieved evidence, and all four published answers preserve the source-relative clock and the unknown calendar year in context. The initial receipt remains unchanged.

## Source and retained meaning

The source supplies three linked temporal propositions:

1. “next year” is measured from the original record;
2. the original record is undated, and the reference must not be replaced by today or processing time;
3. consequently, the calendar year cannot be recovered.

The retained prose says “next year, relative to the undated source record rather than today or processing.” That wording retains the premises that make the calendar year unrecoverable. Its typed temporal qualification independently records `precision: unknown` and `clock_relation: undated`. This is not a case where prose merely says “next year” and leaves the anchor implicit. Because the anchor is explicitly an undated record and present/processing substitution is expressly excluded, no definite calendar year can be derived from the retained statement.

The source's final Russian sentence is an explicit consequence and useful clarification. In this exact context, omitting a separate sentence that repeats the consequence does not change the temporal envelope. Requiring the exact “cannot be recovered” formulation would turn a semantic requirement into a wording requirement.

## Published rows

The English answers at packet coordinates q2 and q4 repeat the retained formulation: “relative to the undated source record rather than today or processing.” They therefore preserve the unknown reference year.

The Russian answers at q5 and q7 say `в следующем году ... относительно недатированной исходной записи, а не сегодняшнего дня или момента обработки`. Although `относительно` is slightly less idiomatic than `отсчитывая от`, the whole clause still binds “next year” to the undated original record and explicitly rejects present-day and processing-time anchors. It does not supply a calendar year or imply that one is recoverable.

Accordingly:

- retained-set usefulness and qualification remain true;

## Residual ambiguity

A reader may find an explicit sentence such as “the calendar year cannot be determined” clearer, especially in Russian. The shorter wording nonetheless semantically entails unknown calendar identity here because it states both the undated source anchor and the exclusion of substitute clocks. The judgment is specific to that complete wording and does not license an unanchored “next year” or typed `precision: unknown` by itself.
