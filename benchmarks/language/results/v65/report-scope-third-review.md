## Independent judgment

The retained report and all six nonnull answers are source-entailed **and**
preserve the material personal nonverification qualification. I do not find a
qualification-completeness error in this record.

The retention does omit the Russian source's exact clause `а не проверенное
мной условие` (“not a condition verified by me”). That lexical omission would
be material if the remaining text merely called the report unconfirmed or
failed to say who lacked which evidence. Here it does more:

> Ada Marlow has not read the service terms and has no independent confirmation of Bo Winters's report that [the terms permit the specified service action].

The possessor of the epistemic limit is Ada, its object is Bo's exact
service-terms report, and the measurement-versus-replacement restriction stays
inside that object. In this context, Ada's “no independent confirmation” means
that she has no basis independent of Bo's report establishing the reported
condition. Coupled with her explicit statement that she has not read the terms,
it preserves the source contrast: these are Bo's words relayed by Ada, rather
than a contractual condition Ada personally established.

The Russian source clause is adjectival and contrastive: `Это слова Bo Winters
в моём пересказе, а не проверенное мной условие.` It does not describe a
separate attempted check, a failed verification event, or a second object. It
clarifies the epistemic status and scope of the same relayed proposition. The
retained sentence preserves that status through explicit outer/inner
attribution plus two Ada-specific limits. Repeating the source's exact
“not personally verified” wording would be clearer, but it is not necessary for
semantic completeness in this particular sentence.

## Entailment versus completeness

Truth entailment alone is the easier conclusion: none of the retained or answer
wording asserts that the terms are correct, that Ada verified them, that a
shipment was booked, or that a measurement occurred. The record preserves only
Bo's report that the terms **permit** sending the device for measurement.

The completeness question turns on whether “no independent confirmation of the
report” retains the explicitly personal condition-verification limit. I judge
that it does because all of the following remain grammatical parts of one
sentence:

- Ada is the person who has not read the terms;
- Ada is the person who has no independent confirmation;
- the confirmation object is specifically Bo's report, not an unspecified
  message or a general state of knowledge;
- Bo's report is specifically about the contractual permission;
- the permitted purpose is return-spring tension measurement, not spring
  replacement.

This attachment rules out the materially broader readings “the report is
unconfirmed by somebody” and “nobody has verified the condition.” It also avoids
replacing Ada's personal limit with note-level or global uncertainty.

There is a real lexical ambiguity in English: in some settings, “independent
confirmation” could be heard narrowly as third-party corroboration, allowing a
person to have checked something personally while lacking a second person's
confirmation. That is not the most natural reading here. The phrase is `Ada ...
has no independent confirmation of Bo's report`, not “Ada has received no
third-party confirmation,” and the same source immediately defines the intended
contrast in Russian. A stricter action-predicate rule could demand both lexical
forms, but applying it here would treat a source-explicit cross-language
clarification as two independent events rather than one qualification. I would
not do so on this evidence.

`не имеет независимого подтверждения` to Ada and `сообщения Bo Winters` to the

Neither makes the condition factual or globally unverified.

The two RU→RU rows are null and therefore do not bear on accepted wording in
this requested review.

## Conclusion

The exact Russian `not personally verified condition` clause would improve
surface explicitness, but its material meaning is already carried by the
personally scoped, proposition-specific absence of independent confirmation,
reinforced by Ada's lack of access to the terms and by the explicit Ada/Bo
reporting chain. I therefore classify the retained record and every nonnull
English/Russian answer as complete with respect to this personal
nonverification qualification, while preserving the English lexical ambiguity
above as a review limitation.
