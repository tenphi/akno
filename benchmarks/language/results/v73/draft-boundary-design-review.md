# V73 draft-boundary design review

## Scope

This note addresses only the two source-faithful Russian counterfactual drafts held at trace answer rows 99 and 104. It does not propose relaxing semantic verification or the correct attribution and coverage-role holds in the same probe.

## Finding

Both drafts form a complete counterfactual unit in ordinary Russian:

- `нереализованный вариант` establishes unrealized scope;
- `при покупке ... был бы покрыт` binds purchase as the unrealized condition and wheel-hub repair as the conditional consequence;
- a following sentence says Ada did not buy the extension;
- the closure says the extension was not her active coverage.

The local `discourse` rejection therefore reflects a bounded recognizer coverage gap. The current accepted complete-unit grammar is sensitive to constituent order and a small set of closure wordings even when all safety-bearing elements remain in one closed unit.

## Smallest safe boundary

Add positive controls for these exact grammatical families rather than a general search for `бы` or `нереализованный`:

1. an attributed or unattributed clause headed by `нереализованный вариант заключался в том, что`;
2. a bounded `при покупке <extension phrase> <repair phrase> был бы покрыт` consequence, allowing the consequence noun phrase before the passive predicate as in the V73 drafts;
3. a following personal or passive nonpurchase sentence referring to the same extension;
4. a final same-subject denial of active coverage.

All four parts must be present in the same bounded sentence unit and closure sequence. Keep existing negative controls for quotation/example framing, retraction, realized wording, missing conditional morphology, changed actor, clause breaks, adversative splicing, missing nonpurchase, and missing inactive-coverage closure. The independent semantic verifier continues to decide object identity, year, actor, and whether the prose actually matches the cited source.

A generation hint may prefer an already recognized canonical construction, but it cannot replace recognizer coverage: natural, complete Russian word order should not be rejected solely for differing from one template. No change is warranted for row 51's protected-name transliteration or row 139's changed coverage roles.
