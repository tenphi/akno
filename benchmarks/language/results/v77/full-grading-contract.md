Judge original-source entailment separately from usefulness and qualification. A retained paraphrase cannot serve as an oracle for its answer. Focused source-entailed subsets may be useful, but coupled actors, objects, tested properties, denial/report chains, epistemic/discourse scope and source-clock limits must travel together. Source-asserted reports can be asserted as reports without upgrading their embedded content to verified world fact. English generated knowledge and each requested answer language are separate obligations; supplied proper names remain exact.

Read-only admission controls have justified retention holds and justified null answers, with no writable coverage credit. Missing cells caused by exceptions cannot be fabricated as nulls or graded as a semantic rejection; report an ungradable observation explicitly if found.
