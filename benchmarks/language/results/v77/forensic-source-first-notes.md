## General grading boundaries

- Complete useful retention means every independently useful proposition requested by the source, including separate action denials and governing epistemic, fictional, hypothetical, counterfactual, or temporal scope.
- A focused answer may select a source-entailed subset. It need not restate a matrix predicate supplied only by the question, but every asserted clause must be supported by its own cited record. Coupled actor, object, property, polarity and qualification cannot be separated from the selected proposition.
- Reports may be asserted as reports while their embedded content remains attributed and unverified. A source saying someone lacks personal knowledge or verification does not establish universal ignorance or lack of evidence.
- Null writable answers are usefulness losses because every writable source supplies an answer. A safe refusal based on incomplete retrieval is not a source-justified abstention. The read-only cases require typed retention holds and null answers and receive no writable coverage credit.
- Identical query/view pairs across answer languages share the same retrieval judgment. Public answer language is independent: ordinary technical vocabulary must be translated, while proper names and `Zephyr QX-100` remain exact.
- Missing cells from an operation exception are ungradable infrastructure failures, not semantic nulls.
