Keep a direct assertion's provenance author separate from its readable actors and reporters when generating and verifying answers. Faithful copies no longer receive a metadata-only requirement to name the author. The independent source reading also binds explicitly named reporters to their reported predicates, so a positive broad verdict cannot excuse an answer-absent reporter or a changed role; source selection, qualification and predicate timing remain required.
