# Blackwater Bay, April 2031

Three days, 10–12 April 2031. [[people/ada-marlow]] and [[people/bo-winters]].

- Flew out on the 10th, back on the 12th.
- Stayed at the Gull & Anchor, 99 EUR a night.
- Ada could not eat at the harbour place — shellfish everywhere. Worth remembering.
- The office Ada now works in is a ten-minute walk from that hotel, which is how the job came up.
