# Letter from the letting agent, February 2031

A source page: quotable, and never a destination for a new claim. Recall returns lines from it with their
addresses, so an agent can cite the sentence rather than paraphrase it.

<!-- source -->

> Dear Ms Marlow and Mr Winters,
>
> Thank you for reporting the boiler fault on 28 January. We have instructed Harrow & Vane to attend, and
> can confirm that the cost of the annual service is borne by the landlord under clause 9 of your agreement.
>
> We would remind you that the notice period under clause 14 is two months in either direction, and that
> written consent is required before any subletting.
>
> Yours sincerely,
> Pell & Rook Lettings
